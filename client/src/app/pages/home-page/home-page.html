§ 01 Introduction

I build software that bridge the gap between machine intelligence and human understanding. My work spans theoretical research into model robustness and the engineering of production-scale ML pipelines.

Currently at the Cleveland Clinic, I mentor a team of junior interns constructing internal applications that support users across multiple departments. In addition to that, I completed my Bachelor's of Science at John Carroll University, with minors in Business Analytics and Data Science.

I believe the most interesting problems live at the edges; where disciplines collide, where assumptions break down, where the obvious answer turns out to be wrong.
